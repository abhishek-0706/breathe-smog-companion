Describe the changes/updates made in the deployed version:
Breathe helps families navigate winter smog with real Open-Meteo/CAMS modeled outdoor air data, household preferences, low-emission routines, indoor-air guidance and community actions. It explains exposure and climate impacts separately, shows data timestamps and uncertainty, and uses a transparent illustrative CO2 calculator. The modular source includes a researched 30-case synthetic golden dataset, 110 passing automated tests and GitHub CI/CodeQL. Added shared safety routing, pediatric fit guidance, integer AQI normalization, safer chat reset/cancellation, local-time checklists, and blocked-storage recovery. Verified 82 browser checks, 30 final production probes and a Lighthouse mobile lab run scoring 100 in performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO. See VALIDATION.json for revision-specific verification scope.

Gen AI services and usage:
Google Gemini powers contextual clean-air chat from normalized household preferences, bounded recent conversation and server-fetched modeled air data. The API key remains in Vercel server environment variables. Safety-critical questions receive labeled sourced guidance; provider fallback is labeled. Open-Meteo/CAMS supplies modeled environmental data and is not GenAI. Generative answers may contain errors and do not replace official alerts or medical care.

Submission administration and raw evaluation details are maintained privately outside the public repository.
